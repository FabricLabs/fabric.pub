The HTTP and Hub edge

JSON at the door. The protocol stays on the other side of it.

The bridge

HTTP accepts JSON. Core peers speak typed fields. examples/message-bridge.js is that translation for a ping and for a sidechain patch. examples/wire-catalog.js in the Hub repository checks that the Hub's names for JSONCall and P2P_CHAT_MESSAGE still match the opcodes in @fabric/core.

Sign, decline, ask

Signer rules sign or decline each request by amount, destination, site, and rate, and they never sign anything they do not clearly allow. A decline wins over a sign for the same scope. A rule with an unknown field is invalid, and while any rule is invalid the Hub asks instead of signing.

Every signing decision is logged. A request no rule settles waits for you to approve. A federation withdrawal needs a maximum amount on the sign rule. Without that cap, it is not signed.

examples/signer-rules.js checks those four outcomes. It does not hold a key.

A price, then a floor

The document-markup script starts from a remote cost and the default markup, 10 percent. A cost of 1000 sats lists at 1100. A price above zero is at least the smallest payment a mainnet HTLC can settle. A cost of zero stays free.
