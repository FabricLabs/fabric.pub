Fabric

Peer-to-peer agreements, settled in Bitcoin.

Coordinate directly

Fabric lets people coordinate and pay each other directly, with Bitcoin as the settlement layer and no company in the middle.

It is a protocol and reference client for those agreements. @fabric/core, @fabric/http, and Fabric Hub are the stack you build on.

@fabric/core v0.1.0 is a release candidate.

Read the protocol

Ten walkthroughs follow one path, from how peers talk to where HTTP and the Hub sit. Each one names an offline script in the matching repository.

The scripts generate keys in memory, check a few facts, and exit. They do not listen, and they do not start Bitcoin or Lightning.

Run Fabric Hub

Fabric Hub desktop installs on macOS, Windows, and Linux. On Mac it needs macOS 13 or later. Setup downloads and manages Bitcoin Core and Core Lightning for you. On Windows, the app manages Bitcoin Core only; Lightning isn't included.

Sign in to Fabric sites with your own key. Passport checks that the request comes from the site your browser actually shows. Pair Passport with a Hub, or with another signer, by comparing a six-digit code.

A public Hub is at hub.fabric.pub.

In use

  • GoonCitizen relays Star Citizen gameplay from your game log to a live dashboard, and only reads the log. Its groups share Bitcoin wallets that need several members to sign.
  • Sensemaker is a self-hosted suite for gathering and analyzing information toward goals you set.
  • Doorman is a community bot for Discord, Slack, and Matrix that welcomes new members and runs onboarding, extended with plugins.
  • Soundtrack is a self-hosted app for listening to music together.
